"Marie-Claude Dubuc, PA-C is a Florida-licensed Physician Associate with more than 15 years of clinical experience across emergency medicine, urgent care, internal medicine, and dermatology. She earned her Master of Medical Science from Nova Southeastern University in Davie, Florida and her Bachelor of Science in Psychology and Biology, Cum Laude, from Hunter College in New York. Marie-Claude has served as Lead PA-C in multiple South Florida emergency rooms including Plantation Hospital, HCA University Hospital in Davie, Westside Hospital, North Shore Medical Center in Miami, and Florida Medical Center in Lauderdale Lakes. She is a longstanding member of the American Academy of Physician Associates, the Florida Academy of Physician Associates, and the Emergency Medicine Society. Fluent in English, French, and Haitian Creole, Marie-Claude founded Weight Loss Davie to bring provider-led medical weight management — including semaglutide and tirzepatide care — to patients in Davie, Florida and across the state."
