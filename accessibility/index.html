Website Policy

Accessibility Statement

Last updated August 19, 2026

Weight Loss Davie aims to provide a website people with diverse abilities and assistive technologies can use.

Our Current Approach

  • Semantic headings and descriptive page titles.
  • Keyboard-visible focus styles and skip links.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Labels and instructions for website forms.
  • Responsive layouts for zoom and smaller screens.
  • Color and contrast choices designed for readability.

Need Assistance?

If any content, form, or feature is difficult to access, call (310) 408-6687 or email contact@weightlossdavie.com. Tell us the page and the assistance you need. We will provide the information through a reasonable alternative when available.

Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. Website changes are reviewed for keyboard access, structure, labels, readable contrast, responsive behavior, and common assistive-technology needs.
