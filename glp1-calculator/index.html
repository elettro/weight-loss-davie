How to Use This Calculator

Quick guidance for choosing your inputs and interpreting your estimate with realistic expectations.

Rx

Medication

Semaglutide usually reflects steadier progress. Tirzepatide often projects a faster pace. Retatrutide is investigational and research-only.

ACT

Activity Level

This helps estimate how efficiently your body uses energy. Higher activity can slightly improve projected pace, and even light activity matters.

PRO

Protein Goal

Protein helps protect muscle during weight loss. Better intake can support body composition and preserve lean mass while fat is reduced.

H2O

Water Goal

Hydration supports energy, digestion, and medication tolerance. Water intake can influence how people feel during a GLP-1 program.

WK

Weeks on Medication

Early weeks can be slower. Progress often changes as dosing increases over time, so this input helps make your estimate more realistic.

RNG

How to Read Results

Results are estimates, not guarantees. You will see a range instead of a fixed promise, and milestones help map the likely path forward.
