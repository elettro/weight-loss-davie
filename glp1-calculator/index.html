How to Read Results

Results are estimates, not guarantees. You will see a range instead of a fixed promise, and milestones help map the likely path forward.

Age, sex, activity, protein, and water inputs support discussion prompts. They do not change the study-informed medication range. Individual results depend on many factors this calculator does not measure.

Evidence and limits

The medication profiles use broad ranges informed by clinical-study results reported in current FDA prescribing information. They do not account for dose, contraindications, side effects, treatment interruption, health conditions, or individual response. BMI is a screening measure and does not diagnose health status.
