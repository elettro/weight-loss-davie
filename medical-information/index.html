Medical Information and Sources

Updated August 19, 2026

Semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescription drug ingredients. Specific branded products have specific FDA-approved indications, dosing instructions, warnings, and contraindications. A licensed clinician must determine whether any treatment is appropriate for an individual patient.

  • NIDDK guide to prescription medications for overweight and obesity
  • NIDDK guide to choosing a safe weight-loss program
  • CDC adult BMI categories and interpretation

How to Use This Information

Use these sources to prepare questions for a licensed healthcare professional. Do not use website information or calculator output to select a drug, determine a dose, assess contraindications, or respond to side effects.

Emergency and Safety Questions

For urgent symptoms or a possible emergency, call 911 or seek immediate emergency care. For medication-specific safety questions, contact the prescribing clinician or pharmacist and consult the current FDA-approved prescribing information.
