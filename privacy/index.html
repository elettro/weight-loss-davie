Website Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated August 19, 2026

This policy explains how Weight Loss Davie collects and uses information submitted through this website. It applies to website activity only. Clinical records and patient communications handled through an approved care process follow separate privacy and recordkeeping requirements.

Information You Submit

The contact form requests your name, phone number, email address, area of interest, and an optional message. Do not submit diagnoses, medication lists, laboratory results, insurance details, or other sensitive medical information through the general website form.

How Information Is Used

  • Respond to your inquiry and explain next steps.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • Measure calls, form submissions, and other website conversions.
  • Meet legal, security, and recordkeeping obligations.

Analytics, Advertising, and Cookies

The website uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Google Ads measurement. These services may receive device, browser, referral, interaction, and approximate location data through cookies or similar technologies. Google provides controls for advertising personalization and analytics opt-out settings.

Form Processing

Website inquiries are processed through Formspree. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to Formspree and delivered to Weight Loss Davie. Review Formspree's privacy terms before using the form if you want more detail about its processing practices.

Sharing and Selling

Weight Loss Davie does not sell personal information submitted through the website. Information may be shared with service providers used to host, secure, measure, or process the website and with authorities when legally required.

Retention and Security

Website inquiry data is retained only as long as reasonably needed for follow-up, operations, security, or legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage method offers absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact Weight Loss Davie to ask about website inquiry information associated with you or to request a correction or deletion where applicable. Browser settings also let you limit cookies.

Contact

Email contact@weightlossdavie.com or call (310) 408-6687 with privacy questions.
