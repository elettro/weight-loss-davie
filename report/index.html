What We Found
On Day One

The site had been recently migrated from WordPress to GitHub Pages. The content existed but the technical foundation was broken in multiple ways that would have actively harmed Google rankings if left unaddressed.

  • ✕
    Zero schema markup No JSON-LD anywhere on the site. Google and AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) had no machine-readable data about what the business is, who runs it, or where it is.
  • ✕
    Broken internal links — 404 errors All nav and footer links contained the old WordPress path /weight-loss-davie/. Google was crawling these, finding 404s, and losing trust in the domain in real time.
  • ✕
    Not indexed by Google Zero pages in Google's index. The site was completely invisible to search engines.
  • ✕
    No sitemap.xml or robots.txt Google had no crawl guidance and no list of pages to discover.
  • ✕
    No Google Search Console No way to monitor indexing, crawl errors, or search performance.
  • ✕
    No meta descriptions Google was writing its own — almost always worse than a crafted one.
  • ✕
    Drug names absent from body copy Homepage only mentioned "GLP-1" generically. Semaglutide and tirzepatide — high-volume search terms — did not appear anywhere in crawlable text.
  • ✕
    No Broward County geo-targeting Site only referenced Davie. Patients from Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Hollywood, and Weston — all within 15 miles — would not find it organically.
  • ✕
    White-on-white text across multiple pages Several pages had invisible body text — white text on white card backgrounds — due to CSS inheritance from dark section parents.
  • ✕
    Phone number format inconsistency Site showed non-clickable phone text in some places and (310)408-6687 in others — NAP inconsistency that hurts local SEO.

What Remains

Path To
9–10 / 10

The remaining gaps cannot be solved with code — they are off-page signals that build over time. Here is what moves the needle from 7 to 9–10:

  • 1
    Directory citations Submit to Healthgrades, Yelp, WebMD Directory, Zocdoc, Facebook Business, Florida DOH provider directory. Each listing = a citation signal for Google and AI models. Target 10+ listings within 30 days.
  • 2
    Google reviews Currently 1 review at 5.0 stars. Review volume directly impacts Map Pack rankings. The Leave a Review button is live. Target 10+ reviews to become competitive in local pack results.
  • 3
    Canonical tags Add canonical tags to every page to prevent duplicate content issues from the WordPress migration period.
  • 4
    Open Graph / social meta tags Add og:title, og:description, og:image so the site previews correctly when shared on social media or via text message.
  • 5
    GMB profile completion Add business hours, at least 5 photos, and list all services in the GMB dashboard. A fully completed GMB profile ranks higher in the Map Pack.
  • 6
    Formspree / form backend Contact form currently has no backend wired to send emails on GitHub Pages. Connect Formspree to route form submissions to contact@weightlossdavie.com.
