What Elettro Implemented

Ten workstreams, completed and verified before launch.

Fixed malformed HTML, duplicate metadata, canonical tags, and broken internal links site-wide.

Rebuilt the XML sitemap around 14 indexable pages, keeping utility and unverified pages out of search.

Added and validated structured data across Organization, Clinic, Provider, FAQ, and page-level schema types.

Published a provider license reference, medical source library, editorial policy, and current review dates.

Reframed medication content around FDA-approved indications for accuracy and compliance.

Rebuilt the treatment calculator as clearly labeled educational ranges, sourced to FDA and CDC data.

Added llms.txt, answer-oriented FAQs, and source panels so AI assistants can cite the site accurately.

Replaced the broken social share image and reduced page weight through modern WebP image delivery.

Added skip links, visible keyboard focus, responsive images, and a working 404 page for accessibility.

Built repeatable audit scripts to keep schema, links, and assets verified before every future deployment.
